# Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Cycling Training (plugin id `victoranto-cycling-coach`) is a set of instructions for Claude. It has no servers, no database, no analytics and no code that sends data anywhere. This policy explains what data the plugin works with and where it lives.

## What the plugin reads

When you use it, Claude may read, through connectors **you** have connected in your own Claude account:

- **Strava:** your activities and their data (power, heart rate, cadence, speed, elevation, GPS-derived distance), your zones and your gear.
- **Stas / Intervals.icu:** your training history and load, your planned calendar, and wellness data such as sleep, resting heart rate, HRV, weight and subjective scores.

It also uses what you type or paste in the conversation: your athlete profile (age, weight, FTP, races, availability, equipment, other sports, injuries or limitations you choose to share), RPE and what you ate during rides.

Some of this is health-related data. Share only what you are comfortable sharing.

## What the plugin stores

The plugin itself stores nothing. Your athlete profile is kept only where you choose, inside your own Claude account:

- in Claude's memory, if you have memory turned on, and/or
- in the instructions of your own Claude Project, if you paste it there.

You can view, edit or delete it at any time from your Claude settings and your Project. How Claude stores conversations and memory is governed by Anthropic's privacy policy: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy

## What the plugin sends

- The plugin sends no data to the author or to any third party.
- It only writes to your Intervals.icu calendar (through the Stas connector) when you explicitly confirm a previewed change.
- Data held by Strava, Stas and Intervals.icu is governed by their own privacy policies.

## Children

The plugin is not intended for people under 18.
